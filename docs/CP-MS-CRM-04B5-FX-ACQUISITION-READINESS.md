## Provider decision
Use Frankfurter v2 with ECB-only filter, two fixed HTTPS pairs USD/PHP and EUR/PHP. Reference rates are daily, not intraday market or bank settlement quotes. USD/PHP is rebased by Frankfurter from ECB data; do not label it as a direct BSP or ECB USD/PHP publication. No blended rates, default rates, automatic capture on render, provider credentials, personal identifiers in provider calls or browser-supplied rates. Both observations must have the same date, no later than UTC retrieval date and no more than seven calendar days old. This accommodates weekends/holidays while refusing unexpectedly stale acquisitions. Older explicitly selected historical snapshots remain valid for reproduction. Midnight UTC encodes the provider calendar date; actual publication time is unknown. Actual first retrieval time remains immutable on replay. Exact PostgreSQL numeric accepts at most eight fractional digits (no silent rounding); provider correction with different rate creates a different immutable version. A provider or network failure writes nothing and never replaces a selected snapshot.

References: https://frankfurter.dev/ ; https://frankfurter.dev/methodology/ ; https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/extensions/http ; https://github.com/pramsey/pgsql-http .
